# Font guidance

Site/editorial headings: Lora Regular (400). Functional text: DM Sans Regular (400), with Medium (500) selectively for navigation, labels, and buttons. Use licensed distributions from the font providers and retain their license notices when self-hosting. No font binaries are included.

The finalized heavier Bodoni Moda wordmark is supplied artwork. Its precise numeric weight is not specified; do not approximate it by typing a logo. Bodoni Moda is not the site heading font. Avenir, Avenir Next, and Raleway belong to the archived system.
