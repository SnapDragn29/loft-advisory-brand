# LOFT Advisory Messaging & Service Architecture

**STATUS: CURRENT / AUTHORITATIVE**  
Last updated: October 2026  
This file controls LOFT Advisory positioning, audience, service architecture, Blueprint terminology, scope boundaries, and preferred messaging. If another active or archived file conflicts with this file on those subjects, follow this file.

## Core Positioning

LOFT Advisory helps physicians plan, build, launch, and establish independent medical practices.

LOFT’s core engagement period is:

**Idea → Feasibility → Build → Launch → First Year**

LOFT is not positioned as a mature-practice growth consultancy, M&A advisor, valuation firm, partnership-transaction advisor, succession-planning firm, or indefinite strategic advisory service.

## Primary Audience

The canonical audience term is **physicians**.

LOFT is not strictly physician-exclusive and may selectively work with other clinician-owners where appropriate, but public-facing copy should generally use physician-centered language rather than broad provider-centered language.

## Core Brand Positioning

Primary public-facing brand line:

**Independent practice, thoughtfully built.**

Approved supporting brand expression:

**Elevating independent medicine.**

“Elevating independent medicine” may be used selectively in brand signatures, philosophical messaging, campaigns, or closing language, but it should not replace the primary positioning statement.

The word **growth** is not prohibited. It may be used naturally when referring to early practice growth, development, team growth, historical experience, or the normal progression of a newly launched practice.

Do not use “growth” to imply mature-practice expansion, acquisition strategy, partnership restructuring, multi-location expansion, succession planning, or indefinite consulting unless such work is separately and explicitly approved.

## Engagement Architecture

LOFT has two primary engagement models.

### Full Practice Launch

A comprehensive engagement supporting physicians from initial vision and feasibility through practice development, launch, and the first 90 days of operations.

Standard professional fee begins at **$15,000+**, billed according to engagement milestones.

Full Practice Launch is the most comprehensive way to work with LOFT.

### Modular Advisory

Focused advisory support for one phase or a defined combination of phases within the LOFT Practice Blueprint.

Modular Advisory is used when a physician does not require the full launch engagement or needs focused assistance in selected areas.

### First-Year Advisory Extension

The First-Year Advisory Extension is **not a third peer engagement model**.

It is an optional continuation for Full Practice Launch clients following the first 90 days of operation and continuing through the end of the practice’s first year.

Standard structure:

- Months 4–12 following practice opening
- $750 per month
- One scheduled advisory meeting per month
- Financial and operational performance review
- KPI and actual-versus-projected review, as applicable
- Patient acquisition and retention review
- Early operational troubleshooting
- Limited routine between-meeting support
- Additional meetings, substantive analysis, implementation work, or new deliverables billed separately
- Ends at the conclusion of the practice’s first 12 months of operations
- Does not automatically renew into indefinite advisory services

The First-Year Advisory Extension continues the work of Phase 14 — Early Operations & Practice Performance.

## The LOFT Practice Blueprint

The LOFT Practice Blueprint is an organizational framework for building an independent medical practice.

It consists of exactly **5 stages and 14 phases**.

The Blueprint is not rigidly sequential. Phases may overlap or occur in a different practical order depending on project needs, dependencies, specialty, market, and practice model.

### 01 Discover

**Phase 1 — Vision, Model & Feasibility Strategy**

**Phase 2 — Business Formation & Professional Advisor Coordination**

**Phase 3 — Funding & Capital Strategy**

### 02 Design

**Phase 4 — Branding & Marketing Foundation**

**Phase 5 — Financial Infrastructure Setup**

**Phase 6 — Regulatory & Compliance Planning Support**

**Phase 7 — Operations Design**

### 03 Build

**Phase 8 — Office Selection & Build-Out Guidance**

**Phase 9 — Technology & Equipment Selection**

**Phase 10 — Staffing & Human Resources Development**

**Phase 11 — Pricing Strategy**

### 04 Launch

**Phase 12 — Marketing & Launch Planning**

**Phase 13 — Patient Acquisition & Retention Strategy**

### 05 Grow

**Phase 14 — Early Operations & Practice Performance**

## Definition of Stage 05 — Grow

Within the LOFT Practice Blueprint, **Grow** means the early growth, stabilization, and performance of a newly launched practice, generally during its first year of operations.

It may include:

- key performance indicator review
- financial performance review
- cash-flow monitoring
- cash-reserve review
- profitability review
- owner-compensation considerations
- actual-versus-projected performance
- patient acquisition and retention performance
- pricing refinements
- staffing adjustments
- workflow refinements
- operational troubleshooting
- first-year performance review

Stage 05 does **not** mean mature-practice expansion.

It does not imply:

- acquisitions
- mergers
- additional locations as a standard service
- partnership restructuring
- succession planning
- mature-practice growth strategy
- indefinite advisory services
- long-term strategic consulting

Phase 14 may be delivered through Full Practice Launch, the First-Year Advisory Extension, or Modular Advisory for a newly launched practice, generally during its first year.

## Scope Boundaries

LOFT’s standard service architecture is centered on helping physicians create and establish independent practices.

Do not infer or advertise the following as standard LOFT services:

- M&A advisory
- practice valuation
- acquisitions
- partnership transactions
- succession planning
- mature-practice expansion strategy
- multi-location expansion as a standard service
- indefinite strategic consulting
- long-term retained growth advisory

Historical experience involving practice growth or expansion may be described accurately in founder biography and credentials. Historical experience does not automatically define LOFT’s current service scope.

## Voice & Copy Rules

LOFT should sound natural, direct, warm, knowledgeable, measured, and professional.

Preserve approved LOFT wording whenever it remains accurate and strong.

Do not rewrite language merely for novelty or stylistic variation.

Avoid generic consulting language, inflated claims, buzzwords, overly symmetrical marketing copy, and phrasing that sounds like an outside branding agency rather than an experienced advisor speaking clearly to a physician.
